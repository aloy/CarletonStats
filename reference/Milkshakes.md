# Milkshakes (chocolate) Nutrional information on chocolate milkshakes from a sample of restaurants.

Milkshakes (chocolate) Nutrional information on chocolate milkshakes
from a sample of restaurants.

## Format

A data frame with 29 observations on the following 11 variables.

- Restaurant:

  Names of restaurants

- Type:

  Type of restaurant, `Dine In` `Fast Food`

- Calories:

  Calories per serving

- Fat:

  Fat per serving (g)

- Sodium:

  Sodium per serving (mg)

- Carbs:

  Carbohydrates per serving (g)

- SizeOunces:

  Size of milkshake (ounces)

- CalPerOunce:

  Calories per ounce

- FatPerOunce:

  Fat per ounce

- CarbsPerOunce:

  Carbohydrates per ounce

## Source

Data collected by Carleton students Yoni Blumberg (2013) and Lindsay
Guthrie (2013).
