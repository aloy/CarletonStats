# Penguin survival by tag type

Survival outcomes for penguins fitted with metal or electronic tags.

## Format

A data frame with 2 observations on the following 3 variables.

- TagType:

  Tag type: `Metal` or `Electronic`

- Status:

  Survival status: `Survived` or `Electronic`

## Source

Saraux, C., et al., “Reliability of flipper-banded penguins as
indicators of climate change,” Nature, January 2011; 469: 203–206.

Lock, Robin H., et al. Statistics: Unlocking the power of data. John
Wiley & Sons, 2025.
